What We Offer

Cleaning Services Built for Toronto Homes

From weekly tidying to deep-clean resets, we handle every corner with care.

Regular Home Cleaning

Consistent, thorough cleaning on a schedule that fits your life. Kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas — everything spotless, every visit.

  • Dusting & surface wipe-down
  • Vacuuming & mopping all floors
  • Kitchen & bathroom sanitizing
  • Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly

Deep Cleaning

A top-to-bottom reset that reaches where regular cleaning doesn't. Perfect for move-ins, move-outs, or when your home needs a serious refresh.

  • Inside appliances & cabinets
  • Baseboards, vents & light fixtures
  • Grout scrubbing & tile detail
  • Move-in / move-out ready

Airbnb / Short-Term Rental Turnover

Fast, hotel-quality turnovers between guests. We work around your booking calendar so you never miss a check-in — and your reviews stay five-star.

  • Linen change & restock
  • Guest-ready presentation
  • Restocking consumables checklist
  • Same-day turnarounds available
